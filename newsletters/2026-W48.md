# Road running weekly: 16–22 Nov 2026

The big story is off the track: Peres Jepchirchir has been banned for three years for EPO, and the Sydney Marathon title passes to Irine Cheptai. Four priority marathons follow on 6 December.

## Top results
- No priority races were run in the last seven days, so there are no results to report. Nothing in the next 10 days either.

## News and stories
- The AIU announced a three-year ban for Peres Jepchirchir, the world marathon champion, after she admitted an EPO violation. Her results since 17 July 2026 are annulled, so the Sydney Marathon win goes to runner-up Irine Cheptai. Reported by a news outlet, not an AIU statement in our files. [Source](https://runningmagazine.ca/the-scene/peres-jepchirchir-handed-three-year-doping-ban-over-epo/)
