# Road running weekly: 9–15 Nov 2026

A quiet week: one priority race, and now a three-week gap before a crowded 6 December, when four priority marathons run on the same day.

## Top results
- **Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Half Marathon** (Platinum, Hong Kong, 15 Nov): Wede Kefale (ETH) won the women's race in 1:09:04, seven seconds ahead of Glenrose Xaba (RSA); Addisu Gobena (ETH) won the men's in 1:01:35, almost a minute clear. Neither time is close to the season's top 100 (1:08:15 and 1:00:21 are the cut-offs), a reminder that a Platinum label does not guarantee fast times. Gobena also won the Sydney Marathon in August.

## News and stories
- Valencia's organisers named a first elite list in July, including John Korir (2:02:24), who won Boston in April, and Fotyen Tesfay, the fastest woman of 2026 (2:10:51 in Barcelona). That list is four months old and may have changed, so check the final start list nearer the race.
