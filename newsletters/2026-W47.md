# Road running weekly: 9–15 Nov 2026

A quiet week: one priority race, and now a three-week gap before a crowded 6 December, when four priority marathons run on the same day.

## Top results
- **Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge Half Marathon** (Platinum, Hong Kong, 15 Nov): Wede Kefale (ETH) won the women's race in 1:09:04, seven seconds ahead of Glenrose Xaba (RSA); Addisu Gobena (ETH) won the men's in 1:01:35, almost a minute clear. Neither time is close to the season's top 100 (1:08:15 and 1:00:21 are the cut-offs), a reminder that a Platinum label does not guarantee fast times. Gobena also won the Sydney Marathon in August.

## News and stories
- **Yomif Kejelcha is out of the Valencia Marathon.** He announced it on Instagram on 13 November and the organisers confirmed it the same day: an injury stopped his build-up; the type of injury and a return date were not given. He ran 1:59:41 on his debut in London in April and was Valencia's headline entry. [Source](https://marathonhandbook.com/kejelcha-withdrawal-valencia-marathon-field/)
- Without him, John Korir (2:02:24, Boston winner in April) leads the men's field. In the women's race, Fotyen Tesfay, the fastest woman of 2026 (2:10:51), faces defending champion Joyciline Jepkosgei, and Agnes Jebet Ngetich, this year's fastest at 10 km, makes her marathon debut. [Source](https://www.marathons.com/en/marathon/yomif-kejelcha-pulls-out-of-valencia-agnes-jebet-ngetich-gets-set-for-her-marathon-debut_m433)
